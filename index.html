01 / The protocol

Built by students.
Upgraded every year.

Techfest is the annual science and technology festival of IIT Bombay, run entirely by students. It began in 1998 and has grown into a three-day gathering of competitions, lectures, workshops and hundreds of exhibitions.

This edition explores one idea: the line between human and machine is a design choice. Scroll on to take the cyborg apart.
